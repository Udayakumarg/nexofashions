Independent Audits

Our Multi-Stage Physical QA Framework

We don't trust empty promises. Nexo Fashions deploys independent inspectors directly to factory floors. We track each batch using three structured physical quality gates:

  • 1

    Inline Raw Material Testing

    We inspect raw fabrics for weight consistency (GSM), yarn counts, dimensional shrinkage (ISO 6330), and color fastness before cutting.

  • 2

    Mid-line Sewing Verification

    Our inline inspectors verify stitch margins, seams, embroidery backing, logo prints, and label tags while sewing is in full progress.

  • 3

    Pre-Shipment AQL Checkout

    Final checkouts are conducted following international single sampling plans (MIL-STD-105E / AQL 1.5 Acceptable Quality Limit) to verify zero defective shipments.
