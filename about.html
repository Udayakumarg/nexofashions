Our Story & Sourcing Philosophy

Bridging the gap between Western high-fashion designs and Tiruppur's advanced sustainable manufacturing plants.

Who We Are

A Local Sourcing Advocate with Global Expectations

Nexo Fashions was founded as an independent buying agency in Tiruppur to challenge the typical transaction-only export model. We realized international apparel brands often struggled with communication barriers, delayed samples, unannounced subcontractors, and compliance tracking.

We set out to serve as a secure, transparent, and highly communicative buying extension for global fashion. Our seasoned merchandisers and quality auditors oversee operations directly on factory floors, ensuring our partners receive high-standard batches every time.

"We don't just find suppliers. We establish sustainable, certified, and cost-controlled supply systems that allow fashion houses to scale with zero risk."

Ethical Standard

Zero-tolerance for child labor, forced shifts, or unfair wages. We strictly partner with factory clusters audited by BSCI, SEDEX, and OEKO-TEX standard bureaus.

Competitive Advantage

Why Global Sourcing Managers Partner with Us

  • ✔

    On-Floor Quality Inspections

    We don't wait for final delivery. Our QA teams run regular raw yarn checks, inline seam audits, and final AQL 1.5 checkpoints directly inside the mills.

  • ✔

    Bespoke Fabric Engineering

    Struggling with specialized textures? We help engineer bespoke French Terry, heavy organic jersey, interlocks, ribs, and high-stretch performance blends.

  • ✔

    Consolidated Shipping Management

    We coordinate items from multiple small production runs into single container loads, reducing port handling and ocean transit bills.
