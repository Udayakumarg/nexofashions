Our Sourcing Journey

From initial design sheets and CAD prototyping to on-floor audits and customs clearing. How we manage your collections.

Operational Excellence

The 8 Sourcing Gateways

We break down apparel procurement into clear, traceable steps to ensure complete visibility at every milestone.

Gate 01

Sourcing Inquiry (RFQ Intake)

Your journey starts by sharing sketch files, detailed tech packs, material specs, target price points, and certification needs via our secure RFQ portal. This helps us align with your product segment.

Gate 02

Material & Construction Consultation

Our textile engineers analyze your specs to optimize raw cotton selections, knit weave construction, print methods, and seam parameters to deliver high durability and budget efficiency.

Gate 03

Audited Factory Matchmaking

We match your design sheets and volumes with audited factories from our vetted Tiruppur network. We ensure selected suppliers hold updated BSCI, SEDEX, or GOTS certifications.

Gate 04

Physical Proto Sampling

We produce physical mockups (Fit-samples, Lab-dips, and Size-sets) on actual factory sewing machines, checking fits, alignments, and color fastness before cutting fabric.

Gate 05

Cost Engineering & Scaling

We structure pricing contracts directly with primary mills, utilizing local cluster benefits and raw cotton scale to offer optimized FOB unit costs.

Gate 06

Production Oversight

Once bulk orders are filed, our merchandisers monitor knitting, soft-flow dyeing, high-density screen printing, and tailoring lines to keep production on track.

Gate 07

Rigorous QA Inspection

Nexo Fashions' independent quality assurance controllers run inline raw checks and final pre-packing inspections following strict international AQL 1.5 standards.

Gate 08

Export Dispatch & Clearance

We coordinate packing lists, oversee container stuffing, manage customs filings, and book ocean or air shipping to deliver products securely to your ports.
