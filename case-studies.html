Case Study 01: Streetwear Sourcing

European Streetwear Brand | Heavyweight loopback Hoodies

The Challenge: A fast-growing street couture brand in Scandinavia needed to source a premium heavyweight hoodie line (450 GSM pure loopback French Terry). Local suppliers in Europe had high fabric costs, while typical Asian exporters demanded a minimum order of 3,000 Pcs per color, which exceeded their startup inventory plans.

The Solution: Nexo Fashions mapped their design requirements to a specialized boutique garment factory in Tiruppur. We engineered custom heavy cotton yarns, monitored mineral dye washes to guarantee soft touch feels, and managed flatlock sewing parameters to secure structural durability.

Case Study 02: Sustainable Supply Chain

US Sustainable Fashion Brand | Traceable Organic Line

The Challenge: A sustainable label in California required absolute proof that their cotton knitwear collection was GOTS organic certified. They had encountered local suppliers who failed to provide transaction certificates (TCs) at crucial spinning and eco-dyeing gateways, exposing them to compliance audits.

The Solution: Nexo Fashions placed their orders with a GOTS-certified vertical spinning and sewing network in Tiruppur's green corridors. Our compliance merchandisers monitored and collected official Transaction Certificates (TCs) for every production run, ensuring complete traceability.
