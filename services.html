Our Sourcing Solutions

Providing Western apparel brands with a complete, structured procurement footprint directly inside India's textile capital.

Capabilities #1

Fabric Sourcing & Engineering

Our procurement teams maintain direct lines with raw yarn spinning mills and advanced processing houses. We engineer custom fabrics, ensuring strict quality checkouts on dye penetration, weights, and shrinkage specs.

  • ✔ Procurement of 100% GOTS Organic Cotton, Supima, Bamboo, and recycled poly blends.
  • ✔ Development of heavy French Terry, interlocks, ribs, thermal weaves, and active piques.
  • ✔ Dye matching via spectrophotometer scanning, ensuring delta E values stay below 0.5.
Capabilities #2

Product Development & Sampling

We translate brand design files and flat layouts into working fabric dimensions, creating accurate physical mockups on actual factory sewing lines to lock fits and specifications before cutting.

  • ✔ Fast CAD pattern drafting, resizing, grading, and digital fabric layout markers.
  • ✔ Rapid Fit-sample, Size-set, and Pre-production sample (PPS) delivery.
  • ✔ Technical design consultations to optimize seam structures, stitches, and print margins.
Capabilities #3

Rigorous Quality Assurance (QA)

Nexo Fashions deploys dedicated quality checking controllers to follow your orders through the production floors. We execute strict inline testing and pre-packaging random audits to catch minor sewing, print, or size issues.

  • ✔ Pilot-run tests of the first 100 pcs to lock cutting press molds and print screens.
  • ✔ Inline inspections checking panel stitches, stitch density (SPI), and neck trim tension.
  • ✔ Final pre-shipment inspections following international AQL 1.5 / 2.5 standards.
Capabilities #4

Logistics, Stuffing & Custom Clearance

Managing shipping documents, certificate clearances, and sea freight bookings can be challenging. We coordinate items from multiple audited factories, stuff them into single container volumes, and handle local custom clear ports.

  • ✔ Consolidated packing lists and shipping manifests across different suppliers.
  • ✔ Secure, verified container stuffing oversight to prevent shipping box crumpling.
  • ✔ Booking and booking management with top ocean/air container carriers to secure competitive freight rates.
